# Generation 1 Updated Renders (RENDERS-GEN-1)

This directory contains the newly updated, fully verified renders for both **Port A** (Transatlantic Passenger & Rail Harbour) and **Port B** (Iron Shipbuilding Yard & Graving Docks).

All 19 renders reflect the architectural and structural overhaul addressing:
1. **Solid, Watertight Roofs**: All see-through / culled roofs have been replaced with solid Welsh roofing slate, interior ceiling boarding, horizontal eaves ceiling plates, and raised iron/timber clerestory ridge monitors with lead flashing.
2. **Anchored Hydraulic Swing Bridge**: Rebuilt across the 32-meter lock entrance channel at Trafalgar Dock with a 6.8 m diameter cylindrical granite pivot pier, resting abutments embedded into the quay walls, heavy hogback through-trusses, and continuous standard gauge railway tracks directly supported by solid ground and deck.
3. **Articulated Bowstring Landing Stage Bridges**: Heavy bowstring lattice through-trusses spanning from dressed granite shore abutment pylons (with trunnion pillow blocks and pivot pins) across an intermediate floating pontoon bent to the floating stage with flanged steel roller carriages running on trackways.
